The Problem

Every community sees it. Nobody owns it.

🌱

Overgrown Spaces

Bushes and weeds become security risks. But who coordinates the cleanup?

🗑️

Uncollected Waste

Markets choked with trash. No clear way to hire hands fast.

🚨

Safety Gaps

A handful of troublemakers make streets unsafe for months. Organising a response is hard.

💰

Coordination Friction

No easy way to pool resources or fairly compensate people willing to work.

These aren't invisible problems—people see them every day. The gap is that there's no easy way to pool resources, coordinate action, or fairly compensate the people willing to actually do the work.

How It Works

Four simple steps to get things done

1

Post a Task

Spot a problem? Post it. Clear overgrown bushes. Clean the market. Organise a neighbourhood watch.

2

Pool Donations

Community members who care donate sats. Money sits safe in a multisig wallet—no single person controls it.

3

Volunteers Claim Work

Residents show up, do the work. Proof of completion is documented.

4

Instant Payment

After verification from multisig keyholder consensus, volunteers get paid instantly via Lightning. No waiting.

Why Bitcoin & Lightning?

Instant Payments

Lightning means volunteers don't wait days for payment. Finish work, get paid in seconds. No intermediary takes a cut.

Mobile-First

Kenya already trusts digital money. Lightning works on any phone, no bank account needed.

Safe Funds

Multisig wallets mean 3-of-5 trusted keyholder consensus before any payout. No single person can run with donations.

Community Owned

Bitcoin is trustless. No corporation, no government, no permission needed. Just community members coordinating.
